Strength Sessions

Push · Pull · Legs — focused, heavy, efficient

🔵 Push Day

  • Bench Press — 4×6–8 (heavy, progressive)
  • Incline DB Press — 3×10
  • Seated Shoulder Press — 3×10
  • Lateral Raises — 3×15
  • Tricep Dips or Pushdowns — 3×12
  • Push-ups to failure — 2 sets (finisher)
Chest/shoulders/triceps. Lead with the heavy compound. You're doing this twice a week — first session go heavier, second session you can drop weight slightly and focus on form/pump.

🔷 Pull Day

  • Weighted Pull-ups or Lat Pulldown — 4×6–8
  • Barbell or DB Row — 3×10
  • Cable Row or Seated Row — 3×10
  • Face Pulls — 3×15 (posture + rear delts)
  • Bicep Curls — 3×12
  • Hammer Curls — 2×12 (finisher)
Back/biceps. Pull-ups are your priority — if you can do weighted, do it. Face pulls are non-optional: they fix posture and help you look taller in photos.

🟣 Legs Day

  • Barbell Squat or Goblet Squat — 4×8
  • Romanian Deadlift — 3×10
  • Leg Press — 3×12
  • Walking Lunges — 2×10 each leg
  • Leg Curl — 3×12
  • Standing Calf Raises — 3×15
Legs once a week is fine given your run volume. Don't skip it — legs drive metabolism and the squats/deadlifts burn more calories than any other session.
Progressive overload rule: Log your weights. Each session, try to add 2.5–5kg or 1 extra rep on your main compound lift. If you can do all reps cleanly, go heavier next time. This is what drives body recomposition in 3 weeks.

Daily Core — Elevated

Every session ends with this. 15 minutes. No skipping.

This replaces the lighter Phase 1 core. You've built the base — now we make it count. Cycle through A/B/C to avoid adaptation.

Core A — Anterior
  • Plank — 3×45 sec
  • Ab Wheel / Hollow Hold — 3×30 sec
  • Hanging Knee Raises — 3×15
  • Sit-ups — 3×20
  • L-sit hold (parallettes or dip bars) — 2×20 sec
Core B — Obliques
  • Side Plank — 3×40 sec each
  • Russian Twists — 3×20 (weighted)
  • Cable Woodchop or Band Rotation — 3×12 each
  • Bicycle Crunches — 3×20 alt reps
  • Copenhagen Plank — 2×20 sec each
Core C — Posterior
  • Superman Hold — 3×15
  • Bird Dog — 3×12 alt reps
  • Glute Bridge with pause — 3×20
  • Good Mornings (light) — 3×15
  • Dead Bug — 3×10 each side
Rotation: A → B → C → A → B → C etc. Cycle through them. Each targets a different part of the core and they all build toward that tight, strong mid-section look in photos.

Nutrition — Deficit Mode

Target 2–3kg in 3 weeks · Protein first · Don't starve

🥩 Protein — Non-Negotiable

~2g per kg bodyweight every day. Every meal has a protein anchor. Without this, you'll lose muscle not fat — and look worse, not better.

⚡ Carb Timing

Carbs before and after training only. Oats or banana pre. Rice/sweet potato post. Cut refined carbs on rest days. This alone creates a meaningful deficit without feeling deprived.

💧 Hydration + Deficit

3L+ daily. High training volume + deficit = easy to get dehydrated. Electrolytes if sweating a lot. Well-hydrated skin and muscles = better photos.

🍳 Solo House Advantage

No compromises on food. Batch cook Sunday. Greek yoghurt + eggs every morning. Prep protein for the week. You have full control — use it.

📉 The Deficit

~500 cal/day deficit = ~0.5kg/week. But with your training volume, you'll exceed this. Don't drop below 1800 cal on training days — you'll tank energy and muscle. Aim for 2000–2200 cal on hard days.

😴 Sleep = Fat Loss

You actually have this now. Dark room, no baby at 2am, 7–8 hrs. This is your biggest advantage. Poor sleep halts fat loss even with perfect diet and training. Protect it.

🛁

Recovery — Don't Skip This

High volume only works if you recover between sessions

🧖 Sauna Protocol

  • Post-weights or post-swim: 15–20 min
  • Not before a hard session
  • Dramatically cuts DOMS on a high-volume week
  • HRV trick: if you feel wrecked, go sauna not gym

♨️ Contrast Therapy

  • Cold pool → warm spa → repeat
  • 5 min hot / 1 min cold × 3 rounds
  • Best on Saturday after long run
  • Reduces inflammation, improves sleep

💤 Sleep — Your Edge

  • Target 7–8 hrs with the house to yourself
  • Magnesium glycinate before bed
  • Dark, cold room
  • No phone 30 min before sleep

📆 When to Scale Back

  • Resting heart rate +8–10 bpm? Easy day
  • Sharp joint pain → swap to swim or rest
  • Feeling flat? Add carbs, not more training
  • Max 10% volume increase week-on-week
