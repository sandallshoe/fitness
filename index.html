Nutrition Strategy

Combat skinny-fat · Support training · New-parent practical
Core Principles

🥩 Protein First

Aim for ~1.6–2g per kg bodyweight. Every meal should have a fist-sized protein source. This is the #1 lever for body recomp (losing fat, gaining muscle simultaneously).

⚡ Carbs Around Training

Eat your main carbs in the meal before and after workouts. Oats or fruit pre-workout. Rice/sweet potato/pasta post-workout. Reduce refined carbs on rest days.

💧 Hydration

2.5–3L water daily — more on swim days. Start with a big glass before morning training. Electrolytes matter if you're sweating heavily. Limit alcohol (but don't stress pre-wedding toasts).

🥦 Veg Volume

Fill half your plate with veg at lunch and dinner. This keeps you full on fewer calories while micronutrients support recovery and energy. Frozen veg is fine — easy with a baby.

😴 Sleep = Nutrition

Poor sleep tanks testosterone, spikes cortisol and causes fat retention. Getting 6–7 hrs (even broken) matters as much as diet. Nap when Rory naps when possible.

🍳 Practical Eating

Batch cook on Sunday. Greek yoghurt + berries for breakfast. Pre-made protein shakes for when life is chaos. Don't skip meals — blood sugar crashes wreck energy and willpower.

Sample Day (Training Day)
  • Pre-workout (6:30am): Banana + black coffee, or small bowl oats with milk
  • Post-workout breakfast (8:30am): 3–4 eggs + toast or Greek yoghurt + granola + fruit
  • Lunch (12:30pm): Chicken/tuna/leftover protein + rice or wrap + salad
  • Afternoon snack: Protein shake or handful nuts + apple
  • Dinner: Protein + veg + small portion carbs (fish, steak, chicken thighs work great)
  • Evening: Avoid heavy eating after 8pm. Chamomile tea, magnesium supplement can help sleep

Rest & Recovery

Your secret weapon — and the rec centre makes it easy

🧖 Sauna (rec centre)

  • 10–15 min after weights or swim
  • Improves recovery, reduces soreness
  • Heat exposure = some cardiovascular benefit
  • Don't do pre-intense workout

♨️ Spa / Cold-Warm Contrast

  • Alternate between cool pool & warm spa
  • Excellent for reducing inflammation
  • 5 min hot → 1 min cold × 2–3 rounds
  • Great stress relief for new-parent brain

💤 Sleep Strategy

  • Even 6 hrs beats 4 hrs significantly
  • Dark, cool room
  • No phone for 30 min before bed
  • Magnesium glycinate supplement: proven for sleep quality

📆 When to Skip & Adapt

  • Rory has a bad night? → Do the easy home option (20 min walk/stretch)
  • Fiona needs help? → Don't stress, tomorrow is another day
  • Knees hurt? → Pool running or bike instead
  • Feeling great? → Add volume, but max +10% per week
